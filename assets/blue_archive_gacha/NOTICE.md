# Blue Archive recruitment UI assets

`Background.png`, `New.png`, `Point.png`, and `Star.png` are carried over from
the recruitment renderer in [yeci226/BA-discord-bot](https://github.com/yeci226/BA-discord-bot).
That project's package metadata declares the ISC license. Peto ports the visual
composition to Pillow while keeping its own SchaleDB data, rates, collection,
currency, and Recruitment Point logic.

Blue Archive and its visual assets belong to their respective rights holders.
This simulator is an unofficial community feature and is not affiliated with
NEXON Games or Yostar.
